# Content, Data, and Truthfulness Rules

## Non-negotiable
This concept must feel extremely complete without pretending that unverified information is factual.

### You MAY treat these as current context
Use `02_VERIFIED_FACTS.json` as the source of truth for public facts checked on 2026-09-18.

### You MUST NOT invent
- founding year
- founder/family story
- branch count beyond verified locations
- ownership of any domain
- exact opening hours unless independently rechecked or owner-confirmed
- prices not currently verified
- testimonials/review quotes
- revenue or ROI claims
- direct ordering/delivery capability
- business certifications
- dietary/allergen claims beyond what is actually verified

## Demo copy rule
When a field would require owner confirmation, use intentional language rather than fake data.

Examples:
- Good: `Price available on ordering partner`
- Bad: inventing `₹199`
- Good: `Our Story — to be completed with Maosaji's verified history`
- Bad: `Serving Bilaspur since 1917` without owner confirmation
- Good: `Hours to be confirmed for production`
- Bad: guessed opening hours

## Demo data
All dashboard metrics, enquiries, analytics, or sample leads must carry an obvious `Demo data` label.

## Reviews
Do not fabricate named reviews. A section may link to Google/Zomato/Swiggy or use neutral social proof language without quoting invented customers.

## Images
For the private concept, public Maosaji imagery may be used as temporary reference material when technically/legal-use appropriate for a private sales prototype.
For production, replace it with owner-approved originals.
Do not stretch low-resolution imagery. If adequate brand imagery is unavailable, use high-quality food photography as explicitly generic placeholder photography rather than falsely representing it as Maosaji's own photo.

## Domain
Never imply that Aditya owns or should own the client's domain. The production registrar account must belong to Maosaji.
