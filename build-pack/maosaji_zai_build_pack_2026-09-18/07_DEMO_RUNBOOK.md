# 2-Minute Demo Runbook

## Before entering
- Open the site on mobile.
- Keep the homepage preloaded.
- Keep an offline screen recording as backup.
- Confirm admin login works.
- Confirm one demo enquiry can be submitted.
- Confirm item availability toggle works.
- Confirm noindex/private disclaimer is visible.
- Never present the concept as official.

## Sequence
### 0:00 — Homepage
Say: `This is what someone searching Maosaji could arrive at.`

Show:
- hero
- premium imagery
- restaurant/sweets/bakery/namkeen organization

### 0:20 — Menu
Open `/menu`, search `dosa`.
Say: `A customer does not have to hunt through a long PDF or third-party listing to find what they want.`

### 0:40 — Outlet/order path
Choose SVM or Mangla.
Show ordering-partner buttons.
Say: `I am not proposing that you replace Zomato or Swiggy in phase one. This becomes the official discovery and brand layer and routes customers correctly.`

### 1:00 — High-value enquiry
Open Bulk Orders.
Submit DEMO: Corporate gifting / 200 boxes.
Say: `The website is not only a menu. It can capture structured high-value enquiries.`

### 1:20 — Admin reveal
Open `/admin`.
Show the enquiry.
Say: `And this is what your team sees.`

### 1:35 — Menu control
Set a demo item unavailable.
Return to public menu.
Show the change.
Say: `Your team does not need to call a developer every time an item or price changes.`

### 1:55 — Ownership
Say: `The production version lives on a domain owned by Maosaji, with the website, data, outlets, menu and enquiries under your control. Zomato and Swiggy remain ordering partners, not your entire digital identity.`

Stop. Ask questions. Do not continue feature-dumping.
